About me

A bit about me

I’m in Year 12 in London. My main interests are astrophysics, particle physics and programming (and other tech stuff), plus drumming and hiking. I started programming when I was seven and it’s still the hobby I spend the most time on. I also take a lot of interest in AI including AI safety.

Running is my favourite sport. I also play drums, spent several years doing LAMDA and read quite a lot of science fiction. The Bobiverse books and Stephen Hawking’s writing are particular favourites.

I also have a cat called Dusty, who has his own website...

Volunteering, the online kind

Got something technical you need help with?

If there’s something technical you want doing, get in touch and we can talk about it. I’m happy to volunteer for charities, community groups and other small organisations. Mostly that means websites and web apps, like this site and Namesake, but also Python tools and small desktop apps.

Anything big, small, long or short term: a whole website, a fix, a tool, or a second pair of eyes on something broken. Just tell me roughly what you need.
