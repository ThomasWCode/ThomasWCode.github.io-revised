Things I’ve helped with

Volunteering

I build websites and tools for charities and local groups, and I advise a research team on using AI. I’ve also helped in person at Islington parks events and my local Mind charity shop.

Things I’ve built for people

Online and open source

Namesake

I contribute to Namesake, a non-profit that publishes free name change guides and tools for trans people in the U.S. Five of my changes were accepted into its code in my first week, including a fix to the map on its homepage, and I’m now doing some research for the project, which is still in progress.

Advising

Helping a research team use AI

Weekly calls · age 16

A TB vaccine modelling team at LSHTM

What it is

A team at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine that builds mathematical models of TB vaccines. It’s the team my dad works in, and they asked me.

What I do

Weekly calls about bringing AI into how they build the backend of their model: good AI practice, GitHub practice, and how to write prompts.

Hands-on · around Islington

Parks and local events

I’ve helped the Islington Council Parks team at the Grow Show, Apple Day and the Spring Festival at Gillespie Park. Knowing everyone who runs these events makes it hard to get away!

At different events I welcomed visitors, made apple juice and catalogued show entries. I’ve also popped up in Islington Life and the Islington Gazette a few times.

Volunteering, the online kind

Got something technical you need help with?

If there’s something technical you want doing, get in touch and we can talk about it. I’m happy to volunteer for charities, community groups and other small organisations. Mostly that means websites and web apps, like this site and Namesake, but also Python tools and small desktop apps.

Anything big, small, long or short term: a whole website, a fix, a tool, or a second pair of eyes on something broken. Just tell me roughly what you need.
